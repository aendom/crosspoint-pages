Reading direction

How to put it on the reader

  1. Convert and download the file.
  2. Copy it to the SD card, for example to a /comics folder.
  3. On the reader, open it from Browse Files.

Everything happens in your browser: the file is never uploaded. Files of 100 MB or more convert fine on a computer. On a phone, especially an iPhone, the browser may run out of memory with large files, so use a computer for those.

XTCH or XTC?

Both are CrossPoint's own page formats: each page is stored ready to display, so pages turn without the reader having to lay anything out. They differ in how many shades each pixel can have.

XTCH · 4 graysrecommended

Each pixel can be white, light gray, dark gray or black. Shading, screentones and photos look close to the original. Files are about twice as large, and turning a page takes a moment longer.

XTC · black and white1-bit

Each pixel is pure black or white, and grays are simulated with a fine dot pattern. Files are smaller and pages turn faster. Best for line art, text and high-contrast comics.
